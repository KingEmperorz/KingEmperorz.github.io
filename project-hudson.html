Simulation Focus

Built around operations.

Project Hudson is designed as more than a train ride. The goal is a complete operating environment.

01

PA-5 Operations

Realistic train systems, destination displays, propulsion behavior, cab interaction, and operating controls.

02

Detailed Stations

Stations and tunnels built to prioritize recognizable architecture, proportions, materials, and atmosphere.

03

PANSYS

An employee and operations ecosystem for authentication, duty status, permissions, personnel data, and staff functions.

04

ATO / Signaling

Operational logic inspired by modern train control, speed restrictions, safe spacing, and route management.

05

Staff Roles

Engineer, conductor, dispatch, PAPD, HR, and executive functionality designed around controlled permissions.

06

Living World

Passengers, ambience, city detail, day/night behavior, and additional systems that make the railway feel occupied and alive.
